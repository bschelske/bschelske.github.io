Status: PhD Candidate finishing Fall 2026

Currently seeking roles in Machine Learning, Analytical Chemistry/R&D, or Biotech.

I'm a graduate student at Iowa State University completing my PhD in Analytical Chemistry. As a member of the Anand group, I bridge the gap between wet-lab chemistry and artificial intelligence, specifically implementing machine learning for single-cell analysis and developing platforms for cancer detection.

Click here! to learn more about my graduate research or read my publications on Google Scholar (20 citations 😎).

Through my research, I've developed expertise in:

  • Analytical chemistry
  • Machine learning
  • Microfabrication and microfluidics

Outside of research, I love learning new skills and picking up new hobbies. I especially enjoy coding projects and working on this webpage.

If you have any questions, want to chat about research, or just want to say hi, feel free to email me or connect with me on LinkedIn!
